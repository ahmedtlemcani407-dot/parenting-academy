Last updated: October 1, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Parenting Academy collects and how it's used. It's written in plain language on purpose.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us directly.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use this only to respond to you.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we may collect basic, non-identifying technical information when you visit — things like your browser type, device type, and which pages you view. This helps us understand what content readers find useful. We do not collect your name, address, or other personal details just from you browsing the site.

**Cookies.** This site may use a small number of cookies to remember basic preferences (such as a dark/light theme choice). If we run advertising in the future (for example, Google AdSense), those advertising partners may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

## How we use information

- To respond to your messages
- To understand which content is helpful so we can make more of it
- To keep the site running and secure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

## Third-party services

If this site displays ads through a network like Google AdSense, that network may collect data according to its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review Google's privacy policy if you have questions about how they handle data.

## Children's privacy

This site is written for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us at the address on our contact page.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Significant changes will be noted on the site.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our contact page.
